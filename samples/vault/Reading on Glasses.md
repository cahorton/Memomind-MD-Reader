# Reading on Glasses

Back to [[Home]].

## Scrolling

Look up past the threshold to move one page down; look down to move one page
back. Hold your head there and it keeps paging every second and a half.

## Following links

Turn your head right to highlight the next link, left for the previous one.
Double-click to open it and long-press to return. See [[Glossary#Dwell]] for
what *dwell* means, or read the [[Projects/Field Notes]].

## Pausing

Single-click to pause head scrolling and recentre.
